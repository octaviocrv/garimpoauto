Key brand rules:
- Colors: Teal (#0F766E) with Preto Carbono (#0A0A0A) and Branco/Off-White (#F5F5F4) base, Ouro Garimpo (#FBBF24) for specials
- Fonts: Zen Kaku Gothic New (display), Plus Jakarta Sans (data), Inter (body)
- Mascot: Han — 3D Pixar-style Asian male character, see assets/
- Tone: "Amigo do povo", direct, friendly, Brazilian Portuguese casual
- Never: car clichés, corporate cold, neon, heavy screens
